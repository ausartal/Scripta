## SECTION 1: BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Core Voice Archetype

**"The Creative Mentor"**

Not:
- ❌ Corporate consultant (too formal)
- ❌ Tech bro startup guru (too casual)
- ❌ Academic professor (too distant)

Yes:
- ✅ Experienced creative who genuinely wants to help
- ✅ Speaks your language, not jargon
- ✅ Knowledgeable but approachable
- ✅ Excited about possibilities, realistic about outcomes
- ✅ Collaborative, not condescending

### Tone Variations by Audience

**For Students/Learners:**
```
→ Encouraging & Inspirational
→ Curious and exploratory
→ Validates their ambitions
→ Practical and actionable
→ Removes barriers ("You CAN do this")

Example:
"Your ideas matter. Let's turn them into 
something you'll be proud to show the world."

NOT: "Leverage our cutting-edge solutions to 
maximize your learning outcomes."
```

**For Educators/Teachers:**
```
→ Collaborative & Partnership-driven
→ Evidence-based & outcomes-focused
→ Respectful of pedagogical expertise
→ Solutions-oriented, not theoretical
→ Empowers their teaching, doesn't replace

Example:
"Engage your students with real projects. 
See improved outcomes. Keep the control."

NOT: "Revolutionize your classroom with AI 
technology disruption."
```

**For Creators/Entrepreneurs:**
```
→ Ambitious & Energetic
→ Understands hustle & constraints
→ Direct & efficient
→ Celebrates their vision
→ No fluff, pure value

Example:
"Your idea deserves to exist. Let's 
build it faster than you thought possible."

NOT: "Optimize your entrepreneurial journey 
with our suite of integrated solutions."
```

**For Enterprise/Organizations:**
```
→ Professional & Reliable
→ ROI-focused
→ Risk-aware (we understand stakes)
→ Partnership-minded
→ Clear communication

Example:
"Proven track record with institutions 
like yours. Measurable results. Dedicated support."

NOT: "Enterprise-grade solutions with 
scalable architecture and deployment flexibility."
```

### Tone Guidelines

**Always:**
- ✅ Be specific (not vague marketing speak)
- ✅ Lead with benefit (not feature)
- ✅ Use active voice (we do, not it can be)
- ✅ Be honest about constraints
- ✅ Show personality without trying too hard
- ✅ Make it about THEM, not us
- ✅ Use contractions (feels more human)
- ✅ Keep sentences short & scannable

**Never:**
- ❌ Use jargon without explaining
- ❌ Make claims you can't back up
- ❌ Oversell or overpromise
- ❌ Condescend or patronize
- ❌ Use corporate-speak ("synergize," "leverage")
- ❌ Create false urgency
- ❌ Put ourselves before the customer
- ❌ Sound desperate

### FAQ Format

**Question-driven, answer-focused**

```
Q: Will my website be mobile-friendly?
A: Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive 
   and optimized for mobile. Most visitors browse 
   on phones—yours will work perfectly.

Q: Can I update the content myself?
A: Absolutely. We'll show you how (or we can 
   update it for you). No tech knowledge required.

Q: How long does a website take?
A: Typically 6-10 weeks depending on complexity. 
   We'll give you a realistic timeline before 
   we start.

Q: What if I need changes after launch?
A: We include 30 days of free support. After that, 
   we offer hourly support or maintenance plans.

Q: Will my website be secure?
A: Yes. SSL certificate included, regular backups, 
   security monitoring. Your data is safe.
```

Why: Direct questions people actually have
     Honest, specific answers
     Builds confidence
     Shows we've thought ahead
```

## SECTION 10: COPYWRITING CHECKLIST

Before publishing any copy:

**Clarity**
- [ ] Can a first-time visitor understand what we do?
- [ ] Is every word doing work (no fluff)?
- [ ] Are sentences under 20 words (mostly)?
- [ ] Would a 15-year-old understand this?

**Benefits > Features**
- [ ] Are we selling benefits (what it does for them)?
- [ ] Or listing features (what it has)?
- [ ] Does the reader know why they should care?

**Tone**
- [ ] Does it sound like us (not corporate)?
- [ ] Is it appropriate for the audience?
- [ ] Is it honest (no false claims)?
- [ ] Does it feel human, not robot?

**Specificity**
- [ ] Do we have actual numbers (not "many")?
- [ ] Are we naming specific things (not generic)?
- [ ] Would a competitor call us out as vague?

**Calls to Action**
- [ ] Is there a clear next step?
- [ ] Does the button text match the action?
- [ ] Is urgency appropriate (not fake)?

**SEO Consideration**
- [ ] Does the headline include relevant keywords?
- [ ] Are subheadings structured (H1 > H2 > H3)?
- [ ] Do meta descriptions answer the visitor's question?

**Brand Voice**
- [ ] Does this sound like Scripta?
- [ ] Is it consistent with other pages?
- [ ] Would our team feel proud to say this?
